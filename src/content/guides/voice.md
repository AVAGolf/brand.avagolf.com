## Three traits

Every sentence a golfer reads passes all three. The examples are shipped copy.

| Trait | Means | Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plain | One idea per sentence, in the words a golfer uses on the course. No engineering or marketing vocabulary. | “Your round at Pebble Beach is in: 78. See what cost you.” | “Your performance data has been ingested and processed.” |
| Grounded | A claim says what it rests on, and thin data says so before the number. Nothing is promised; what happened is reported. | “Putting is measured on part of your rounds.” | “Official Handicap Index®” |
| Pointed at the next step | A reading ends in what to do about it, for this golfer. | “3 holes on your round have putts with no position. Place them to unlock putting stats.” | “Your putts have no positions.” and nothing after it |

## Data and improvement

- A number names itself for what it is. A handicap AVA Golf calculates is “Round Average” (“Round Avg.” compact); the peer benchmark is “Peer Avg”; `Handicap Index®`, `H.I.™` and `Low H.I.™` (with its date) only on values the USGA issued, one decimal.
- A pattern in the data is an issue, never a diagnosis. An issue states the cost, the pattern, then the evidence: “You are losing strokes off the tee due to a frequent right miss (slice).”
- A recommendation names the part of the game, the strokes at stake and one thing to work on. Videos are “matched to your issues”, never recommended by a system: “Not general tips. Each video targets what your data shows about your game right now.”
- Who speaks: “AVA Golf” when the product read, found or flagged something (“AVA Golf reads every round, range session and simulator swing you already record”); “we” when the team did something (“We received your support request”). Never “the system”, “the algorithm” or “AI”.
- The golfer owns the data. Coach copy says so in those words: “They keep their own data, you get read access plus notes.” To the golfer the coach is “your coach”; on coach surfaces the golfer is a “student”.
- A partner is named only on the surface that connects or manages it (`Settings → Connections`, its connect modal, its connected and failed emails). Everywhere else write “your device”, “a tracker”, “your launch monitor or app”, “a source of rounds”.

## Naming

| Thing | Write | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The product | `AVA Golf`, both words, on every surface including push and email | Ava Golf, AVA, ava |
| The installed app | “the app” (“A new message, when you are away from the app.”) | the mobile app, the iOS app |
| Coach side | “coach mode” and “player mode”, lowercase; “Switch to coach mode” | Coach App, Coach Mode |
| Capture | “Record screen”, “hands-free capture”, “Place your putts” for the putting capture | Ghost Mode (not in shipped copy) |
| Swing work | “Swing Analyzer” as the product area; “swing player”, “swing report”, “live lesson” lowercase | Swing Player, Live Lesson |
| App areas | Capitalised when they name the tab: Home, Activity, Improve, Performance, Journal, Trends, Connections, `Settings → Coach` | the activity page |
| Practice | “practice session” or “range session” (“Your practice session is in”; “My range session on AVA Golf”) | workout, drill session |
| USGA | “USGA account”, “Handicap ID”, “Ratings” as a heading; `Course Rating™`, `Slope Rating®` | GHIN account, GHIN number, Course and Slope Rating |
| A reader without a first name | Drop the name: “What AVA Golf found in your game” | “Hi golfer,” |

## Banned phrases

| Never | Write instead |
| --- | --- |
| AI-powered, powered by AI, machine learning, our algorithm | “AVA Golf reads your rounds”, “from the patterns in your own data” |
| revolutionary, game-changing, unlock your potential, cutting-edge, seamless | The thing that changed, in numbers: “2.1 strokes per round better than before” |
| guaranteed, will lower your handicap | What the data showed so far |
| Official, verified, certified before a USGA mark | The mark alone |
| Estimated Handicap, Skill Level, Handicap Avg | Round Average, Round Avg., Peer Avg |
| Diagnosis, diagnose | issue, “what’s going wrong” |
| ingest, pipeline, process (as a verb to the golfer) | read, import, pull in |
| Exclamation marks, emoji | A full stop |
